
# CHRONOLOGICAL DOCUMENT NOTES AND QUOTES
---
- **Pre-1897**: Mill operations establish industrial use patterns in area
- **1897**: Westwood incorporates as separate town from Dedham WikipediaMassachusetts Municipal Association
- **1830s-1890s**: Mill worker housing at Mill and High Streets corner
- **1909**: Walker Atlas of Westwood [1](https://photos.app.goo.gl/dGd4qK8mQ3KHRZhJ8), [2](https://photos.app.goo.gl/F59V1uUJVdf56aRJA), [3](https://photos.app.goo.gl/wV5UozXdRJNb84mF8), [4](https://photos.app.goo.gl/JUQwoV4jD3LhWHxB6)
- **1927**: [Sanborn Fire Insurance map](https://photos.app.goo.gl/gq1mGWVL2xtZhj546)
- **1941**: [Sanborn Fire Insurance map](https://photos.app.goo.gl/eJNAs6zZW9NxaKck7)
- **1960s-1970s**: Massachusetts implements Inland Wetlands Restriction Act
- **1964** – Initial development of the 7.56-acre estate (future 44 Mill St). 
- **April 24, 1974**: Wetland restriction deed recorded for "Wetland Area #44" under ownership of Joseph F. and Myra R. Hearne
- **April 24, 1979**: Restriction Order recorded by the state under M.G.L. c. 131 (40A) that regulates and restricts the inland wetlands for purposes of preserving flood plain areas, water resources, wildlife, etc. 
- **1980s**: Massachusetts ceases new wetland restrictions Buzzardsbay
- **1989**: Westwood adopts Wetlands Protection Bylaw (Chapter 392) eCode360
- **1 May 1989**: $90 k deed transfer that enlarged the holding to ~7 ½ acres. 
- **2000**: Open Space and Recreation Plan leads to Westwood Land Trust formation
- **2005**: Perry Property preservation includes Mill Street parcels
- **2018 – 2019**: Subdivision creating the ~4-acre parcel now mapped as 28-013 (Lot 2B / 42 Mill St). 
- **November 16, 2023**: Historic Daniel Draper house destroyed by fire wcvb
