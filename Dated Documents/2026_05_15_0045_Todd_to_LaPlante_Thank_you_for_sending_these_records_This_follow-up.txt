Thank you for sending these records. This follow-up is submitted pursuant to G.L. c. 66 §10.
I do not see anything in the records provided that explains the technical basis for the change from the August 25, 2016 SORAD to the December 19, 2016 FORAD.
In response to the applicant's submitted delineations, which did not show the run-of-river Riverfront Area through the impoundment, MassDEP's SORAD found that the Mill Brook impoundment was run-of-river and subject to riverfront regulation. Less than four months later, MassDEP issued the FORAD using delineations that had not been updated to reflect the SORAD's run-of-river finding. MassDEP approved in the FORAD the same delineations it had just found inaccurate in the SORAD, and the records provided contain no technical explanation for the reversal.
What I am trying to locate is the MassDEP record explaining how and why that happened.
Please confirm whether MassDEP has produced all records responsive to my request. Specifically, does MassDEP have any technical memo, analysis, finding, email, correspondence, staff note, or other record explaining the basis for adopting a FORAD plan that omitted the Riverfront Area delineation through the impoundment after the SORAD found that the impoundment was run-of-river?
Please also confirm whether MassDEP's search included the OADR case file for Docket No. WET-2016-022, which adjudicated the SORAD appeal that produced the FORAD.
If MassDEP's position is that the run-of-river issue was deferred rather than changed, please provide the record stating that.
If MassDEP has no record explaining the technical basis for the change, please state that in writing.
