Following up on my letter to DEP yesterday, I wanted to flag something from the October 2022 settlement agreement.

Section 5 states that the dam repair obligation "shall remain enforceable by the Commission against Delapa and his successors and assigns."

However, I could find no record of the settlement agreement at the Norfolk County Registry of Deeds. Without it recorded against the property, a subsequent purchaser would not be bound by it.

If the property transfers before that obligation is secured on the title or otherwise assumed, the SOC remains in place and the dam repair obligation is lost.

If that happens, Delapa may be in breach of the settlement by transferring the property free of an obligation the agreement says must remain enforceable.

I wanted to make sure you were aware since the window to act may be short.

Thank you, Todd
