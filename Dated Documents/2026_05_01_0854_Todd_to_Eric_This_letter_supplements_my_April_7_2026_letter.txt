Dear Mr. Worrall,

This letter supplements my April 7, 2026 letter regarding 42 Mill Street, to which I have not yet received a written response. With the dam repair NOI #338-0756 denial now under appeal with the DEP, the attached recorded documents raise a separate, independent regulatory issue that does not appear to have been addressed in the current record, and that applies whether or not the denial is sustained on appeal.

On February 1, 1974, the Commissioner of the Department of Natural Resources issued a Wetland Restriction Order under G.L. c. 131 §40A. It was recorded at Norfolk County Registry of Deeds in Book 5033, Page 256, on April 24, 1974, with accompanying plans recorded as Plan No. 309 of 1974. The order designates 48 wetland areas in Westwood as restricted under §40A. Wetland Area #44 is one of them. The recorded plan depicts Wetland Area #44 as the Mill Brook impoundment at 42 Mill Street, which is the subject of dam repair NOI #338-0756.

Paragraph 4 prohibits any person from filling, placing, or dumping any material on the wetland; from draining, excavating, or dredging the wetland or removing material from it; and from performing any act that would substantially alter existing patterns of water flow or the natural and beneficial character of the wetland. Paragraph 3 enumerates permitted uses, each conditioned on approval under G.L. c. 131 §40. Whether any of those provisions authorizes the proposed dam repair or removal, and whether the existing §40 approvals satisfy that condition, are questions the DEP should address before issuing or relying on any SOC.

On the NOI for dam repair (#338-0756), the applicant answered "NO" when asked whether the property is subject to a Wetland Restriction Order. The same answer appears in the 2020 NOI that led to SOC #338-0706. The recorded order appears to contradict those answers.

The §40A restriction is independent of the ordinary §40 NOI/OOC/SOC review process. It has been recorded since 1974 as a land restriction affecting the mapped wetland areas. If Wetland Area #44 applies to the Mill Brook impoundment or proposed work area, then the restriction must be addressed independently of SOC #338-0706 and independently of the FORAD issues raised in my April 7 letter.

On April 24, 2026, a worker placed survey stakes and spray paint at the 42 Mill St house build site and posted a sign reading "MA DEP File #338-0706." I include this because the recorded §40A restriction may bear not only on NOI #3380756, but also on any current reliance on SOC #338-0706.

I ask the DEP to address in writing:
1. whether the recorded 1974 §40A Wetland Restriction Order applies to the work proposed at 42 Mill Street, including the dam repair addressed by NOI #338-0756 and the home construction addressed by SOC #338-0706;
2. whether that work falls within any permitted use listed in paragraph 3; and 3. whether the existing §40 approvals satisfy the paragraph 3 requirement for §40 approval.
