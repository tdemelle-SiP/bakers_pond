Dear Ms. Provencal,
Thank you for sending the April 28, 2026 withdrawal letter for DEP File #338-0756.
I am writing to supplement my April 7, 2026 letter regarding 42 Mill Street, DEP Files #338-0594, #338-0706, and #3380756, to which I have not yet received a written response.
Your confirmation that no appeal was filed and that the applicant withdrew NOI #338-0756 makes the questions raised in my April 7 letter more urgent, not moot.
The withdrawal leaves no active permitting path for the dam-related work while the applicant's April 28 letter asserts continued reliance on SOC #338-0706. That creates the precise concern raised in my April 7 letter: the applicant is attempting to preserve the benefit of the 2022 SOC while eliminating the only active filing that returned the site to public review under current conditions.
Additional issues also bear directly on continued reliance on SOC #338-0706.
First, the 2020 NOI identifies Mill Brook as the Riverfront Area resource, but it does not appear to address Mill Brook's Coldwater Fish Resource status or analyze whether that status affects review of the proposed work.
Second, Mill Brook's Coldwater Fish Resource status makes the unexplained removal of the run-of-river classification more consequential, not less. The August 25, 2016 SORAD treated the impoundment as run-of-river based on specific physical characteristics of Mill Brook. The December 19, 2016 FORAD then removed that classification without any record explanation I have found. Where the affected waterway is also a Coldwater Fish Resource, MassDEP should identify the technical basis, if any, for removing the run-of-river finding before allowing continued reliance on SOC #338-0706.
Third, the 2020 NOI appears to have answered "NO" to the Wetland Restriction Order question despite the recorded 1974 G.L. c. 131 §40A Wetland Restriction Order affecting Wetland Area #44.
Before any work proceeds under SOC #338-0706, I ask MassDEP to address the following in writing:
1. Whether MassDEP considers SOC #338-0706 presently reliable and enforceable given the recorded 1974 §40A Wetland Restriction Order.
